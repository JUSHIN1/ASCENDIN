How buying stocks works
When you buy a stock on Ascendin, you purchase units that represent a share of that company's value. The price moves with demand: more buyers push it up, more sellers push it down. Your units sit in your portfolio; you can hold them or sell them back. Value can go up or down, so only invest what you can afford to have tied up.

How bonds work
A bond is a loan you give to a government or company for a fixed period, in exchange for regular interest. Unlike stocks, the price does not swing with daily trading: you agree on a rate and tenure upfront, then get your principal back plus interest at maturity.
